Our Baking Heritage & Story

Founded in 2018 by head baker Clara Lindqvist, North Star Bakery grew out of a passionate desire to bring historic European hearth-baking traditions back to our neighborhood streets.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts in great soil. We source 100% of our grains directly from certified organic family farms across Minnesota and Wisconsin, stone-ground weekly to preserve natural vitamins, rich flavors, and healthy microbes.

Staff Highlights & Craftsmanship

Clara Lindqvist - Head Baker & Founder

Clara completed her apprenticeship in Gothenburg and Brittany before returning home to share traditional sourdough techniques with her hometown neighbors.

Tomas Vance - Pastry Chef

Tomas specializes in classic French lamination, producing hundreds of golden, airy croissant layers each morning before dawn.
